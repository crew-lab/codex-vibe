### Conclusion
Defect found: `orderTotal` in src/cart.mjs computes tax on the gross subtotal instead of the discounted subtotal.

### Per-input results

Input 1: one line of 1000 cents x 1 with a 10% discount
Expected: tax 180, shipping 499, total 1379
Actual: tax 200, shipping 499, total 1399

Input 2: one line of 2500 cents x 2 with no discount
Expected: tax 1000, shipping 0, total 6000
Actual: tax 1000, shipping 0, total 6000

Input 3: one line of 3000 cents x 2 with a 50% discount
Expected: tax 600, shipping 0, total 5400
Actual: tax 1200, shipping 0, total 6600

### Finding
File src/cart.mjs, function `orderTotal`, line 14: tax is computed as `percentOf(gross, TAX_PERCENT)`, but rule 3 requires tax to be 20% of the discounted subtotal. This causes incorrect tax amounts for discounted orders.
