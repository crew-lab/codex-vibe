### Analysis

#### **Conclusion**
**Defect found**: `orderTotal` in `src/cart.mjs` does **not** compute the total as per README.md rules 1–5. The tax is incorrectly applied to the **gross subtotal** instead of the **discounted subtotal** (Rule 3).

---

### **Per-Input Results**

**Input 1**: One line of 1000 cents x 1 with a 10% discount
- **Expected**: tax 180 cents, shipping 499 cents, total 1479 cents
- **Actual**: tax 200 cents, shipping 499 cents, total 1499 cents

**Input 2**: One line of 2500 cents x 2 with no discount
- **Expected**: tax 1000 cents, shipping 0 cents, total 6000 cents
- **Actual**: tax 1000 cents, shipping 0 cents, total 6000 cents

**Input 3**: One line of 3000 cents x 2 with a 50% discount
- **Expected**: tax 600 cents, shipping 0 cents, total 6600 cents
- **Actual**: tax 1200 cents, shipping 0 cents, total 7200 cents

---
### **Findings**
- **File**: `src/cart.mjs`
- **Function**: `orderTotal` (line 14)
- **Input**: All inputs with `discountPercent > 0`
- **Wrong Behaviour**: Tax is calculated as 20% of the **gross subtotal** (`percentOf(gross, TAX_PERCENT)`), not the **discounted subtotal** (Rule 3: "Tax is 20% of the discounted subtotal").
