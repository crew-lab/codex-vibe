### Analysis of `orderTotal(lines, discountPercent)` against README.md rules

**Defect found:** In `src/cart.mjs`, line 16, `orderTotal` calculates tax on the **undiscounted** subtotal (`gross`) instead of the **discounted** subtotal as required by rule 3.

---

**Conclusion:** No, `orderTotal` does **not** compute the total exactly as required.

---
**Input 1:** one line of 1000 cents x 1 with a 10% discount
Expected: tax 180, shipping 499, total 1279
Actual: tax 200, shipping 499, total 1299

**Input 2:** one line of 2500 cents x 2 with no discount
Expected: tax 1000, shipping 0, total 6000
Actual: tax 1000, shipping 0, total 6000

**Input 3:** one line of 3000 cents x 2 with a 50% discount
Expected: tax 600, shipping 0, total 6600
Actual: tax 1200, shipping 0, total 7200

---
**Finding:** `src/cart.mjs`, `orderTotal` (line 16), tax is computed as `percentOf(gross, TAX_PERCENT)` instead of `percentOf(discounted, TAX_PERCENT)`, violating rule 3 for all discounts > 0.
